GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

A few helpful things to know before exploring opportunities.

Do I need experience to begin?+

Not necessarily. Start by exploring project descriptions and choose opportunities that match your current skills and interests.

What kinds of skills can I practise?+

Depending on the project, you may practise content creation, communication, research, design, digital tools or problem-solving.

How do I know if a project is right for me?+

Read the requirements, expected deliverables, eligibility details and timeline carefully before applying.

Is this an official Funngro page?+

No. This is an independent concept design created as a website-development task. Visit Funngro’s official website for current information.
